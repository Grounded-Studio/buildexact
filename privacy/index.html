Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Short version

  • We don't require an account.
  • Your projects, calculations, quotes, invoices and clients are stored locally on your phone using Hive.
  • We don't sell data. We don't have your data.
  • Payment is handled by Google Play, not us.

What we collect

In the Android app. Nothing by default. If you opt in to a crash report after an app crash, that report (device model, Android version, stack trace) is sent to Google Play Console. No personal data is attached.

On this website. Standard GitHub Pages access logs (IP, user agent, requested page) kept by GitHub. No cookies, no analytics scripts, no tracking pixels on calculator pages.

In Google Play. Google Play tracks installs, uninstalls and in-app purchases per their policies. We see aggregated numbers in Play Console; we don't see individual users.

What we don't collect

  • Your measurements, calculation inputs or results.
  • Your projects, quotes, invoices or client list.
  • Your contacts (BuildExact reads them on-device only to populate client fields; nothing leaves the phone).
  • Your location, camera roll or files outside the app.

Permissions explained

  • Contacts — only when you tap "Pick from contacts" in the client manager. Picks a single contact; nothing is uploaded.
  • Storage — to save branded PDFs you export and to pick a logo from your gallery.
  • Internet — only used by Google Play Billing to verify your PRO purchase, by the Play Store to notify updates, and by `url_launcher` when you tap a link.
  • Camera — only if you snap a logo or signature photo; file stays local.

Third-party processors

  • Google Play (payment, update notifications, in-app review prompt)
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting)
  • Cloudflare (DNS)

No analytics providers (no Firebase, no Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no third-party ads).

Your rights (GDPR)

Because we don't have your data, there is nothing to request, correct or delete at our end. Uninstalling the app removes everything on-device. For questions, email support@metenisweten.app.
