Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Short version

  • No account required.
  • Your projects, calculations, quotes, invoices, clients and photos are stored locally on your phone and never leave your device.
  • The app uses Google Firebase (Analytics + Crashlytics) to understand usage and fix crashes, and Google Play for payment. We don't sell data.
  • Our marketing site (grounded-studio.app) uses advertising cookies, only after you accept them.

What the app collects

  • Analytics & diagnostics (Firebase). The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. These automatically send aggregate usage events (e.g. which features are opened, whether a quote was generated), your device model, OS and app version, a device/advertising identifier and crash reports to Google — only to improve the app and fix bugs. We never attach your name, measurements, projects or client data.
  • Advertising identifier. Firebase uses your device's advertising ID; we also use it to measure whether our Google Ads campaigns lead to installs. You can reset or limit it in Android settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Purchases (Google Play). The one-time PRO purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation only, never your card details.
  • Everything else stays on your device. Measurements, inputs and results, projects, quotes, invoices, your client list, photos, logo and signature are stored locally with Hive and are never uploaded.

What the app does NOT collect

  • Your measurements, calculation inputs or results.
  • Your projects, quotes, invoices or client list.
  • Your contacts (read on-device only to fill a client field; nothing is uploaded).
  • Your location, camera roll or files outside the app.

Permissions explained

  • Contacts — only when you tap "Pick from contacts" in the client manager; nothing is uploaded.
  • Storage / Camera — to save branded PDFs you export and to pick a logo or signature; files stay local.
  • Internet — used by Firebase (analytics/crashes), Google Play Billing, the Play Store (update notifications) and url_launcher when you tap a link (WhatsApp, Maps).

Our website & online services

  • buildexact.app (this site) runs on GitHub Pages. No cookies, no analytics scripts, no tracking pixels. GitHub keeps standard access logs (IP, user agent, requested page).
  • grounded-studio.app (our studio / marketing site) uses the Meta Pixel and Google Analytics 4 (ID G-DBG67CDKSP) to measure our advertising. These load only after you accept the cookie banner, and GA4 IP addresses are anonymised.
  • AI shopping list (browser calculators). If you use this optional feature, your calculation type, title, inputs and results are sent to Google Gemini to build the list. Please don't enter personal data in those fields.
  • Website account. To use credits on the website you log in with a one-time code sent to your email. We store your email address, your credit balance and a log of credit changes on our EU server (Strato). Your login is kept in a secure cookie for up to 90 days. Login attempts (email and IP address) are logged briefly to block abuse. The code email is sent from our own mail server at Strato.
  • Credits (website). Website credit payments are handled by Mollie; we store your payment email and IP address on our EU server (Strato) to deliver and protect the credits. The Android app does not use this — it uses Google Play.
  • Contact form. Messages you send via the contact form are emailed to us (name, email, subject, message and basic device/language info); they are not stored in a database.

Data retention

On-device data lives until you delete it or uninstall the app. Website payment records (email, IP) are kept for our accounting and anti-abuse needs; you can request deletion at any time.

Your rights (GDPR) & deletion

The app has no account and no server-side data, so uninstalling removes everything on-device. For website credit purchases or contact emails, you can request access to or deletion of your data — see our data deletion page or email contact@grounded-studio.app. We action requests within 30 days.

Third parties

  • Google Firebase — analytics & crash reporting (app)
  • Google Play — payment, update notifications, review prompt (app)
  • Google Gemini — AI shopping list (website calculators only)
  • Mollie — payment (website credits only)
  • Meta & Google Analytics — advertising measurement (grounded-studio.app only, after consent)
  • GitHub Pages (hosting), Cloudflare (DNS)
