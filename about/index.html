About BuildExact

BuildExact is a construction calculator app built for builders and DIY across Europe. Made by one developer at Grounded Studio, bootstrapped from the Netherlands.

Why we built it

Every builder we spoke to in NL and BE was juggling the same tools: a dirty notebook for measurements, a WhatsApp group with the client, a laptop at home to type up the quote, and an accountant that nagged about recurring invoices. All of that for a job that was already measured out on day one.

BuildExact collapses that chain. You measure, you tap, you quote, you invoice, you send — on the phone that's already in your pocket. Offline, so a basement or a Belgian field won't stop you.

Our principles

  • Offline-first. No account to sign up, no cloud fetch, no latency between a measurement and a result.
  • Honest pricing. Free core, one-time €7.99 for PRO. No subscriptions. No "trial" that silently charges you.
  • No dark patterns. We don't nag mid-calculation. We don't block exports with a pop-up. We won't send you a "we miss you" email because you don't exist in our database.
  • Built by one person. Which means every mail to contact@buildexact.app gets read by the developer, not a ticket queue.

Our other apps

Grounded Studio also makes FreshGarant (AI-powered food expiry tracker) and Circuit Trace (a minimalist logic puzzle). Everything we build is for the phone in your pocket, built to work without an account.
