What's inside

Everything a builder needs. Nothing you don't.

BuildExact bundles measurement, quoting, invoicing and client management into one offline-first Android app. No subscriptions on the core calculators, no cloud lock-in.

Measurement & calculators

15 trade calculators

Fence, tiles, concrete, insulation, paint, bricks, wood, stairs, drywall, paving, roof, perimeter, area, gravel & sand, cost. Each with per-field tooltips and realistic waste factors.

Shortcuts & favourites

Pin the calculators you use most to a quick-access row on the home screen. Favourite result cards for one-tap reuse.

Calculation history

Every result is kept locally. Scroll back through this week's quotes or last month's measurements without re-entering a thing.

Quotes, invoices & clients

Branded PDF quotes

Add your logo, pick your accent colour, drop a hand-drawn signature, and send the PDF via WhatsApp or email.

Invoices with VAT presets

Dutch 9% / 21% VAT presets, line-items in minutes, mark paid/overdue, export to PDF.

Recurring invoices

Set up weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly templates for maintenance contracts. BuildExact reminds you to send the next one.

Client manager

Pulls contacts from your phonebook. Addresses auto-fill on quotes and invoices. One tap to call, message or WhatsApp.

Company profile

Your KvK/BTW details, bank account and tagline sit in one place — reused on every PDF you generate.

Trade templates

Pre-built line-item lists per trade so you don't type "Arbeidsloon" twice a day. Swipe, tweak, send.

Technical & privacy

Offline-first

Hive on-device storage. Works without a signal. Your data stays on your phone unless you share a PDF yourself.

Dark mode

Follows the Android system theme. Readable in bright sun or at the end of a long day in the van.

No analytics on calculator pages

We measure install success and PRO upgrades. We don't track which numbers you type into the fence calculator.
