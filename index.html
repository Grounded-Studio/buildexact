Why BuildExact

Built for builders who hate paperwork.

Fence, tile, concrete, plaster, flooring, sheet material, steel, excavation, insulation, paint, bricks, drywall, roof, stairs, paving and more. Save any result to a project — all with per-field tooltips and realistic waste factors.

Branded PDF quotes

Your logo, your signature and 5 PDF styles to choose from. Generate a quote on-site, preview it, and send it over WhatsApp before you leave the driveway.

Client manager

Pulls contacts straight from your phonebook. Addresses auto-fill on quotes and invoices. One tap to call or message.

Recurring invoices

Maintenance contracts, monthly cleanouts, retainer fees — set it up once and BuildExact reminds you to send the next one.

Offline-first

Trade templates — ready in seconds

Start a project from 10 trade templates (fence builder, driller, drywall, roofer, painter, flooring, electrician and more). Pre-filled cards, less setup, straight to measuring.

CEO-style start screen with weekly earnings + trend, mini-bar per weekday and an "attention needed" list for overdue invoices and quotes without reply.
