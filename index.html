Why BuildExact

Built for builders who hate paperwork.

Branded PDF quotes

Your logo, your colours, your signature. Generate a quote on-site, preview it, and send it over WhatsApp before you leave the driveway.

Client manager

Pulls contacts straight from your phonebook. Addresses auto-fill on quotes and invoices. One tap to call or message.

Recurring invoices

Maintenance contracts, monthly cleanouts, retainer fees — set it up once and BuildExact reminds you to send the next one.

Offline-first

CEO-style start screen with weekly earnings + trend, mini-bar per weekday and an "attention needed" list for overdue invoices and quotes without reply.
