Common questions

Website or app — which should I pick?

Need something quick? The website is enough (5 free + €2.99 for 50 more). Working daily with projects, quotes and clients? Then the app and one-off €5.99 pays off.

Is PRO really one-time?

Yes. Pay €5.99 once on Google Play and PRO stays unlocked on your account forever, across reinstalls and new phones.

Do website credits expire?

No. Your credits stay in your browser. Credits are stored locally per device — use them whenever.

Can I get a refund?

Google Play handles refunds for the app. You have 48 hours to request one directly from the Play Store. Website credits are instantly usable and non-refundable.

Is my data in the cloud?

No. All data lives locally — on your phone in the app (Hive storage), or in your browser on the website. We don't see your projects, quotes or clients.

What if I only need the calculators?

Then the app-free version is enough: all 15 calculators are fully functional. Only saving more than 2 projects requires PRO.
