Common questions

Is PRO really one-time?

Yes. Pay €5.99 once on Google Play and PRO stays unlocked on your account forever, across reinstalls and new phones.

What if I only need the calculators?

Then stay on Free. The 15 calculators are fully functional. You just can't save more than 2 projects — but you can keep calculating as many times as you want.

Can I get a refund?

Google Play handles refunds. You have 48 hours to request one directly from the Play Store, no questions asked.

Is my data in the cloud?

No. All data lives in Hive on your phone. We don't have your projects, quotes or clients — you share PDFs yourself.
