Delete my data

BuildExact is built to keep your data on your device. Here's exactly how to remove everything.

The Android app

The app has no account and stores your projects, calculations, quotes, invoices, clients, photos, logo and signature locally on your phone (Hive). Nothing is on our servers.

  • Delete everything: uninstall BuildExact. Android removes all local app data.
  • Clear without uninstalling: Android Settings → Apps → BuildExact → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Analytics & crash data (Firebase): this is aggregate and not linked to you by name. To stop it going forward, reset or limit your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, then uninstall.
  • Your PRO purchase is held by Google Play, not us. It stays tied to your Google account so you can restore it; manage it in the Play Store.

Website account

On the website you log in with a code sent to your email. We store your email address, your credit balance with its log, your billing details and, briefly, your login attempts (email and IP address, at most 30 days).

  • Delete it yourself: log in and go to My account → Delete account. Your account, balance, billing details and login data are erased immediately. Remaining credits are lost.
  • What stays: invoices of paid purchases (with the billing details from that moment). Dutch tax law requires us to keep them for 7 years. They are no longer linked to an account.

Contact form

Messages sent through the contact form sit in our email inbox. Want them deleted, or can't log in anymore? Email us and we'll handle it within 30 days.

Request deletion by email

Please send it from your account's email address so we can find the right records.
