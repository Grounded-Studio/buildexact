Delete my data

BuildExact is built to keep your data on your device. Here's exactly how to remove everything.

The Android app

The app has no account and stores your projects, calculations, quotes, invoices, clients, photos, logo and signature locally on your phone (Hive). Nothing is on our servers.

  • Delete everything: uninstall BuildExact. Android removes all local app data.
  • Clear without uninstalling: Android Settings → Apps → BuildExact → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Analytics & crash data (Firebase): this is aggregate and not linked to you by name. To stop it going forward, reset or limit your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, then uninstall.
  • Your PRO purchase is held by Google Play, not us. It stays tied to your Google account so you can restore it; manage it in the Play Store.

Website (credits & contact)

If you bought website credits, we store your payment email and IP address on our EU server to deliver and protect the credits. If you used the contact form, your message sits in our email inbox.

To have this deleted, email us and we'll erase it within 30 days:

Request deletion by email

Please send it from the email address you used, or include your payment ID, so we can find the right record.
