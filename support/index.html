How do I restore PRO on a new phone?

Install BuildExact, open Settings → Entitlement → Restore purchase. Google Play looks up your €7.99 payment and unlocks PRO.

Can I move my projects to another phone?

Projects live locally and don't sync. In the current version you'd rebuild them on the new device. A manual export/import is on the roadmap.

Why isn't the browser version saving my data?

The browser demos are stateless — they don't save anything. For saving, PDF export, client management and recurring invoices, use the Android app.

I found a bug or a wrong formula

Please mail us with: calculator name, the values you entered, the result you got and what you expected. We fix those within days.

Can I request a new calculator?

Yes — we built the fence calculator from a user request. Mail us the trade and the inputs/outputs you'd need, and we'll consider it for the next release.

How do I delete my account / data?

There's no account. Uninstalling the app removes all local data. There is nothing stored on our servers.
