How this calculator works

Core drilling: add a row per unique diameter + depth combo. Pick from standard sizes (52, 82, 102, 132, 162, 182, 202, 250 mm) or type your own. Fill in count and your per-hole price — the app computes total price, total diamond metres (for crown wear) and a time estimate.

Reinforced-concrete toggle: rebar makes drilling 30-50% harder. Toggle on and the app adds +40% time and price automatically. Prevents loss-leader quotes.

Wall / floor sawing: length in metres + depth + price per metre. Same reinforced-concrete toggle available.

Tips for accurate quotes

  • Account for setup time (water hookup, rig assembly) — minimum 1 hour even for a single small hole
  • Natural stone drills slower than concrete — add 20% time
  • For pipe penetrations: always a few mm wider than the pipe + 5mm play
  • Replace diamond crown around 150m drilled — track via the "diamond metres" output
