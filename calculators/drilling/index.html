How this calculator works

Core drilling: add a row per unique diameter + depth combo. Pick from standard sizes (52, 82, 102, 132, 162, 182, 202, 250 mm) or type your own. Fill in count and your per-hole price — the app computes total price, total diamond metres (for crown wear) and a time estimate.

Reinforced-concrete toggle: rebar makes drilling 30-50% harder. Toggle on and the app adds +40% time and price automatically. Prevents loss-leader quotes.

Wall / floor sawing: length in metres + depth + price per metre. Same reinforced-concrete toggle available.

Tips for accurate quotes

  • Account for setup time (water hookup, rig assembly) — minimum 1 hour even for a single small hole
  • Natural stone drills slower than concrete — add 20% time
  • For pipe penetrations: always a few mm wider than the pipe + 5mm play
  • Replace diamond crown around 150m drilled — track via the "diamond metres" output

How the drilling & sawing calculator works

For core drilling, enter the holes with their diameter and depth. The calculator prices each hole, adds up the drilled metres for crown wear and estimates the time. Reinforced concrete gets a surcharge, because rebar slows the drill and wears the crown faster.

For wall or floor sawing, enter the cut length, the depth and your price per metre. The result is a price you can put straight into a quote, together with the total metres for planning the job.

Tip: add a separate call-out or set-up charge for small jobs; one hole takes almost as much preparation as five.

Frequently asked questions

How do you price core drilling?

Usually per hole, by diameter and depth, with a surcharge for reinforced concrete and a call-out charge. Some contractors price large depths per metre.

Why does reinforced concrete cost more?

Rebar slows the drill down and wears the diamond crown faster, so the job takes longer and costs more in tools.

How is concrete sawing priced?

Mostly per metre of cut at a given depth. Deeper cuts cost more per metre.
