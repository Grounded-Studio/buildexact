When to use linear metres

Linear metres (running metres) are used for anything sold by length: skirting boards, cornices, edging, cable trunking, sealant joints and trim. Measure every run and add them together.

Products often come in fixed lengths, for example 2.4 m skirting. Divide the total by the length of one piece and round up, and keep an eye on joints: put them where they are least visible.

Margin tip: add 5–10% for mitres in corners and offcuts.

Frequently asked questions

What is a linear metre?

A length measurement in a straight line, regardless of width. Ten metres of skirting is ten linear metres.

How do I measure skirting boards?

Measure the length of every wall at floor level, subtract door openings and add them up.

How many lengths of 2.4 m do I need?

Divide the total linear metres by 2.4 and round up. 17 m ÷ 2.4 = 7.1, so 8 lengths.
