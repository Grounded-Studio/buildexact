How the plaster calculator works

Enter the area you plaster and subtract doors and windows. Pick the type of plaster: it fills in a typical use per m² per mm and a usual layer thickness. Change both to what your bag says.

The calculator multiplies area × thickness × use, adds waste and rounds up to whole bags. With primer switched on it also gives the litres of primer or bonding agent.

Margin tip: walls are rarely flat. Measure the thickest spot and add 10-15% on old walls.

Worked example: a 30 m² wall

Gypsum plaster on 30 m² of wall with 2 m² of windows, a 10 mm layer, 1.0 kg per m² per mm, 10% waste and bags of 25 kg:

Area to plaster30 − 2 = 28 m²
Plaster28 × 10 × 1.0 × 1.10 = 308 kg
Bags of 25 kg308 ÷ 25 = 12.3 → 13 bags
Primer at 0.15 L/m²28 × 0.15 × 1.10 = 4.6 L

So you buy 13 bags of plaster and a 5 litre can of primer.

Gypsum, lime-cement or skim coat?

Gypsum plaster is the usual choice indoors on brick, block and concrete: light, quick to work and smooth to finish. Lime-cement render is heavier, stronger and copes with moisture, so it is used in bathrooms, basements and on outside walls.

A skim or finish coat is a thin layer of 1 to 3 mm over an existing wall or over plasterboard. It uses little material per m², but needs a wall that is already flat.

Why you need primer

A suction-heavy wall pulls the water out of fresh plaster, which then dries too fast and can crack or come loose. A primer or bonding agent evens out the suction. On smooth concrete use a bonding primer with grit so the plaster has something to grip.

Frequently asked questions

How much plaster do I need per m²?

About 1 kg per m² for every mm of gypsum plaster, so 10 kg/m² for a 10 mm layer. Lime-cement render is heavier: around 1.5 to 1.8 kg per m² per mm.

How many bags of plaster for a wall of 10 m²?

With 10 mm gypsum plaster (1 kg/m²/mm) you need 100 kg, plus 10% waste is 110 kg: 5 bags of 25 kg.

How thick should plaster be?

Gypsum plaster on brick or block is usually 8 to 15 mm, a finish coat 1 to 3 mm. On concrete a thin coat of 3 to 5 mm is often enough.

How many m² does a 25 kg bag of plaster cover?

At 1 kg per m² per mm: 2.5 m² at a 10 mm layer, or about 12 m² for a 2 mm skim coat.

How long does plaster need to dry?

Roughly 1 day per mm of thickness in normal conditions, so a 10 mm layer needs about a week and a half before you paint.
