How the plaster calculator works

Enter the area you plaster and subtract doors and windows. Pick the type of plaster: it fills in a typical use per m² per mm and a usual layer thickness. Change both to what your bag says.

The calculator multiplies area × thickness × use, adds waste and rounds up to whole bags. With primer switched on it also gives the litres of primer or bonding agent.

Margin tip: walls are rarely flat. Measure the thickest spot and add 10-15% on old walls.

Frequently asked questions

How much plaster do I need per m²?

About 1 kg per m² for every mm of gypsum plaster, so 10 kg/m² for a 10 mm layer. Lime-cement render is heavier: around 1.5 to 1.8 kg per m² per mm.

How many bags of plaster for a wall of 10 m²?

With 10 mm gypsum plaster (1 kg/m²/mm) you need 100 kg, plus 10% waste is 110 kg: 5 bags of 25 kg.

How thick should plaster be?

Gypsum plaster on brick or block is usually 8 to 15 mm, a finish coat 1 to 3 mm. On concrete a thin coat of 3 to 5 mm is often enough.
