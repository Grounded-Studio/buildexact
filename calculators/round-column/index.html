How the round column calculator works

Enter the diameter in cm and the height in metres. The volume of one column is π × r² × height; the calculator multiplies by the number of columns and adds waste.

For a concrete pipe switch on Hollow and enter the wall thickness: only the ring is counted. The result shows ready-mix in m³, the number of bags of concrete and the length of form tube you need.

Tip: from about 0.5 m³ ready-mix concrete is usually cheaper and stronger than mixing bags yourself.

Worked example: a column of Ø30 cm

One column of Ø30 cm and 3 m high, 5% waste, bags of 25 kg that make 11.5 litres each:

Cross-sectionπ × 0.15² = 0.0707 m²
Volume0.0707 × 3 = 0.212 m³
With 5% waste0.223 m³ = 223 litres
Bags223 ÷ 11.5 = 19.4 → 20 bags (500 kg)
Form tube1 × 3.00 m, Ø30 cm

Twenty bags is a lot of mixing for one column. With four of these columns you are close to 1 m³, and then ready-mix is the better choice.

Columns, piles and post holes

The same formula works for anything round: a column above ground in a form tube, a pile or pad in the ground, or the concrete around a post. For a post hole, take the volume of the hole and subtract the part of the post that sits in it.

For a concrete pipe or a hollow column only the ring counts. A pipe of Ø30 cm with a 5 cm wall, 3 m long, needs 0.124 m³ including waste, a bit more than half of a solid column.

Bags or ready-mix

A 25 kg bag of dry concrete makes about 11 to 12 litres, so 1 m³ takes some 85 to 90 bags. Mixing that by hand is heavy work and the strength varies from batch to batch. From about 0.5 m³ ready-mix is usually cheaper, stronger and faster. Below that, bags are fine.

Frequently asked questions

How much concrete is in a round column?

π × radius² × height. A column of Ø30 cm and 3 m high: 3.1416 × 0.15² × 3 = 0.212 m³.

How many bags of concrete for a post hole?

A hole of Ø30 cm and 80 cm deep is about 57 litres: 5 bags of 25 kg with about 11.5 litres each.

What is a form tube?

A cardboard tube you fill with concrete to cast a round column or foundation pile. You buy it per metre in the diameter you need.

How many bags of concrete are in 1 m³?

At 11.5 litres per 25 kg bag: 1000 ÷ 11.5 = 87 bags, about 2.2 tonnes.

How much concrete for a hollow pipe?

Outer circle minus inner circle, times the length. Ø30 cm with a 5 cm wall: (π × 0.15² − π × 0.10²) × length.
