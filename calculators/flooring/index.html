How the flooring calculator works

Enter the room size and the roll width. The calculator lays the strips along the longest wall, so you get the fewest seams, and adds a little extra per strip for trimming.

With a pattern repeat each strip is rounded up to a whole repeat, so the pattern lines up at the seams. You see the metres of roll to buy, the purchased m² and the waste.

Tip: a room up to 4 m wide fits a 4 m roll without seams. Then you only buy room length + 10 cm.

Worked example: choosing the roll width

A room of 5 × 4.5 m, 10 cm extra per strip for trimming, no pattern:

Roll of 4 m2 strips of 5.10 m = 10.20 m, 40.8 m² bought
Waste with 4 m40.8 − 22.5 = 18.3 m² (45%)
Roll of 5 m1 strip of 5.10 m, 25.5 m² bought
Waste with 5 m25.5 − 22.5 = 3.0 m² (12%)

The wider roll saves 15 m² of flooring and gives you a floor without a seam. Always compare a few roll widths before you buy.

Strips, seams and the direction of the roll

The calculator lays the strips along the longest wall, so you need as few strips and seams as possible. Each strip gets a few centimetres extra, because walls are rarely straight and you trim the edges in place.

Put seams out of the main walking line and away from the window, where side light shows every joint. Keep all strips in the same roll direction, or the pile of carpet and the sheen of vinyl look different.

Pattern repeat

With a patterned floor every strip has to start at the same point of the pattern. The calculator rounds each strip up to a whole number of repeats, so you lose a little per strip. With a repeat of 64 cm, a strip of 5.10 m becomes 5.12 m.

Frequently asked questions

How many metres of carpet do I need?

Divide the room width by the roll width, round up, and multiply by the room length plus 10 cm. A room of 5 × 3.5 m with a 4 m roll: 1 strip of 5.10 m.

Which roll width should I choose?

The width that covers the room in as few strips as possible. Vinyl and carpet come in 2, 3, 4 and 5 m.

What is a pattern repeat?

The distance after which the pattern repeats. Each strip has to start at the same point of the pattern, so you lose some material per strip.

Do I need underlay under vinyl or carpet?

Under carpet almost always, for comfort and insulation. Under vinyl only if the product says so; many vinyl floors are glued straight onto a smooth subfloor.

How do I measure a room for carpet?

Measure length and width at the widest points, including door openings and alcoves, and round up to the next 10 cm.
