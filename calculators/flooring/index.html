How the flooring calculator works

Enter the room size and the roll width. The calculator lays the strips along the longest wall, so you get the fewest seams, and adds a little extra per strip for trimming.

With a pattern repeat each strip is rounded up to a whole repeat, so the pattern lines up at the seams. You see the metres of roll to buy, the purchased m² and the waste.

Tip: a room up to 4 m wide fits a 4 m roll without seams. Then you only buy room length + 10 cm.

Frequently asked questions

How many metres of carpet do I need?

Divide the room width by the roll width, round up, and multiply by the room length plus 10 cm. A room of 5 × 3.5 m with a 4 m roll: 1 strip of 5.10 m.

Which roll width should I choose?

The width that covers the room in as few strips as possible. Vinyl and carpet come in 2, 3, 4 and 5 m.

What is a pattern repeat?

The distance after which the pattern repeats. Each strip has to start at the same point of the pattern, so you lose some material per strip.
