How to calculate wall area

Wall area is length × height, minus the openings for doors and windows. A wall of 5 m long and 2.6 m high is 13 m²; with a window of 1.5 m² the net area is 11.5 m².

Use the net wall area for paint, plaster, wallpaper and drywall. For a whole room, add up all walls, or use the perimeter of the room × the ceiling height and subtract all openings.

Tip: for paint you can leave small openings in; the edges around them take extra paint anyway.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate the wall area of a room?

Multiply the perimeter by the ceiling height and subtract doors and windows. 18 m × 2.6 m = 46.8 m² minus openings.

How big is a standard door opening?

Often around 0.8–0.9 m wide and 2.1 m high, so about 1.8 m².

Do I subtract windows for wallpaper?

For large windows yes. For small ones many people leave them in, because of pattern matching and waste.
