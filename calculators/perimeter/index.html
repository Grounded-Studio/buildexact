How to calculate perimeter and skirting

The perimeter of a rectangular room is 2 × (length + width). A room of 5 × 4 m has a perimeter of 18 m. That is the length you need for skirting boards, edge strips and expansion joints.

Subtract the width of door openings, because skirting stops at the frame. The calculator adds a cut-waste percentage for mitres and offcuts.

Margin tip: 5% is usually enough; rooms with many corners need a little more.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate the perimeter of a room?

Add the length and width and multiply by two. 6 m and 3.5 m gives 2 × 9.5 = 19 m.

Do I subtract doors for skirting?

Yes. Subtract the width of each door opening, often 0.8–0.9 m.

How do I handle an irregular room?

Measure every wall separately and add them all up, or use the linear metres calculator.
