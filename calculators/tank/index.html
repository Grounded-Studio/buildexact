How the tank volume calculator works

Pick the shape and enter the inside dimensions in cm. Upright round tanks use π × r² × height, rectangular tanks length × width × height.

Enter the liquid level to see how much is in the tank now. For a lying round tank the calculator uses the exact circle segment, because the middle of the tank holds much more per cm than the bottom.

Tip: 1 m³ is 1000 litres. A rain barrel of Ø60 cm and 90 cm high holds about 254 litres.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate the litres in a round tank?

π × radius² × height in dm. A tank of Ø100 cm and 100 cm high: 3.1416 × 5² × 10 = 785 litres.

How much is in a lying tank that is half full?

Exactly half of the capacity. Above or below half the litres per cm change; enter the level and the calculator does the segment maths.

How many litres is 1 m³?

1000 litres. 1 litre is 1 dm³.
