How the steel weight calculator works

Pick a bar diameter, enter the length per bar and the number of bars. The weight per metre is π × r² × 7850 kg/m³: for Ø12 mm that is 0.888 kg/m.

Add a steel plate if you need one: length × width × thickness in mm. With a price per kg you see the cost of all steel at once.

Tip: rebar is often sold per 6 or 12 m bar. Count overlaps of about 40 × the diameter where bars join.

Worked example: 10 bars of Ø12 mm

Ten bars of Ø12 mm rebar, 6 m each, plus one steel plate of 1000 × 500 × 10 mm:

Weight per metreπ × 0.006² × 7850 = 0.888 kg/m
Total length10 × 6 = 60 m
Rebar60 × 0.888 = 53.3 kg
Plate1.0 × 0.5 × 0.010 × 7850 = 39.3 kg
Total92.5 kg

With a price per kg the calculator also shows what all the steel costs.

Why weight matters

Steel is priced and delivered by weight, and the weight tells you whether you can carry it, how many trips you need and what a crane or trailer has to lift. A quick check of the kilos also catches mistakes in a steel list: if the total is far off, a diameter or a number of bars is probably wrong.

Frequently asked questions

How much does rebar weigh per metre?

Ø8 mm 0.395 kg/m, Ø10 mm 0.617 kg/m, Ø12 mm 0.888 kg/m, Ø16 mm 1.58 kg/m and Ø20 mm 2.47 kg/m.

How do I calculate the weight of a steel plate?

Length × width × thickness in metres × 7850. A plate of 1000 × 500 × 10 mm weighs 39.3 kg.

What is the density of steel?

About 7850 kg per m³ for normal construction steel.

How many metres of Ø10 rebar are in 1 tonne?

1000 ÷ 0.617 = about 1620 metres, or 270 bars of 6 m.

How heavy is a 12 m bar of Ø16 rebar?

12 × 1.578 = 18.9 kg.
