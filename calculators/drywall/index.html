How the drywall calculator works

Choose ceiling, wall or partition and enter the area. The calculator divides the area by the size of one board, adds cut waste and rounds up to whole boards. A partition counts both sides.

Screws, joint compound and tape are calculated from the area. In partition mode you also get the vertical studs (one every stud spacing, plus one) and the floor and ceiling tracks.

Margin tip: 10% extra on boards is normal. Rooms with many corners, sockets and openings can need 15%.

Frequently asked questions

How many drywall boards do I need?

Divide the area by the board size. A wall of 4 × 2.6 m is 10.4 m²; with 120 × 260 cm boards (3.12 m² each) that is 4 boards, plus waste.

What stud spacing should I use for drywall?

60 cm is standard for 12.5 mm boards on walls. Ceilings and thinner boards often need 40 cm.

How many screws per drywall board?

Around 30 to 35 screws for a standard board on a wall, with one every 20–25 cm. Ceilings need a little more.
