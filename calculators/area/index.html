How to calculate area in m²

Area is length × width. Measure both sides in metres and multiply: a room of 5 m by 4 m is 20 m². That number is the starting point for flooring, tiles, paint and almost every material order.

For L-shaped rooms, split the room into rectangles, calculate each one and add them up. Measure at floor level and round up to the next centimetre; walls are rarely perfectly straight.

Tip: add the waste percentage for your material afterwards, for example 10% for tiles or laminate.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate square metres?

Multiply the length by the width, both in metres. 3.5 m × 2.8 m = 9.8 m².

How do I measure an L-shaped room?

Divide it into two rectangles, calculate the area of each and add them together.

Should I measure in cm or metres?

Either works, as long as you convert to metres before multiplying. 350 cm × 280 cm becomes 3.5 m × 2.8 m.
