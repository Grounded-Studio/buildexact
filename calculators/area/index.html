How to calculate area in m²

Area is length × width. Measure both sides in metres and multiply: a room of 5 m by 4 m is 20 m². That number is the starting point for flooring, tiles, paint and almost every material order.

For L-shaped rooms, split the room into rectangles, calculate each one and add them up. Measure at floor level and round up to the next centimetre; walls are rarely perfectly straight.

Tip: add the waste percentage for your material afterwards, for example 10% for tiles or laminate.

Worked example: an L-shaped living room

Split the room into two rectangles of 6.0 × 4.0 m and 3.0 × 2.5 m, add 10% waste for laminate and buy packs of 2.2 m²:

Part 16.0 × 4.0 = 24.0 m²
Part 23.0 × 2.5 = 7.5 m²
Total24.0 + 7.5 = 31.5 m²
With 10% waste31.5 × 1.10 = 34.65 m²
Packs of 2.2 m²34.65 ÷ 2.2 = 15.75 → 16 packs

So you order 16 packs. Buying by the pack, never by the exact m², is why the waste percentage matters.

Measuring mistakes that cost money

Measure at floor level, not at skirting or worktop height, and measure each wall at both ends: older rooms are often a few centimetres out of square. Use the larger figure. To check if a room is square, measure both diagonals. If they differ by more than 1 to 2 cm, the cuts along one wall will taper.

Don't forget alcoves, bay windows and the floor inside a cupboard if the floor runs through. Only subtract what will really stay uncovered, such as a fixed kitchen unit or a built-in bath.

Convert to metres before you multiply. 350 cm × 280 cm is 9.8 m²; multiplying the centimetres gives 98,000, which is cm².

Triangles and circles

A triangle is base × height ÷ 2, handy for gable walls and odd corners. A circle is π × radius²: a round patio of 3 m across is π × 1.5² = 7.07 m². Mix shapes in the calculator and it adds them up for you.

Working in feet and inches

Switch the calculator to Imperial at the top of the form and enter feet. A room of 16 × 13 ft is 208 sq ft; with 10% waste you buy 229 sq ft of flooring. To convert by hand: 1 m² is 10.764 sq ft, and 1 sq ft is 0.0929 m².

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate square metres?

Multiply the length by the width, both in metres. 3.5 m × 2.8 m = 9.8 m².

How do I measure an L-shaped room?

Divide it into two rectangles, calculate the area of each and add them together.

Should I measure in cm or metres?

Either works, as long as you convert to metres before multiplying. 350 cm × 280 cm becomes 3.5 m × 2.8 m.

How many square feet are in a square metre?

1 m² is 10.764 sq ft. A room of 20 m² is about 215 sq ft.

How much extra flooring should I buy?

5 to 10% for laminate or vinyl laid straight, 15% when laid diagonally, and always round up to whole packs.

How do I measure a room with a bay window?

Measure the main room as a rectangle. Treat the bay as a trapezium: (front width + back width) ÷ 2 × depth, and add the two together.
