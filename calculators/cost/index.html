How the cost estimator works

Add a line for every part of the job: materials, labour, hire and transport. Each line is quantity × unit price. The estimator adds them up, applies an optional discount and adds VAT at the rate you enter.

Use it for a quick price check or a first estimate for a client. For a quote with your logo, the client's details and a signature, build it in the BuildExact app and share it as a PDF.

Tip: list labour as separate lines, so a client can see what the work costs versus the materials.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make a quick construction estimate?

List every material and every task with a quantity and a unit price, add them up, then add VAT. Keep a contingency for surprises.

Which VAT rate should I use?

The rate that applies in your country for this type of work. Some countries have a reduced rate for certain renovation work; check the local rules.

Should I include a contingency?

For renovation work, yes. 10–15% is common, shown as a separate line rather than hidden in the prices.
