How the roof pitch calculator works

Enter the rise and the run of the roof in centimetres. The pitch angle follows from the ratio between the two, and the slope length from Pythagoras. Multiply the slope length by the roof width and you have the roof area.

Choose tiles, bitumen, EPDM or metal for the material estimate. For tiles you also get battens (rows by batten spacing) and ridge tiles (about 3 per metre of ridge). Cut waste is added on top.

Margin tip: 10% for a simple gable roof. Hips, valleys and dormers need 15% or more.

Worked example: one side of a gable roof

A rise of 300 cm over a run of 450 cm, a roof 10 m wide, concrete tiles at 10 per m², 10% waste:

Pitchatan(300 ÷ 450) = 33.7°
Slope length√(3.0² + 4.5²) = 5.41 m
Roof area5.41 × 10 = 54.1 m²
Tiles54.1 × 1.10 × 10 = 595 tiles
Ridge tiles10 m × 3 = 30

A gable roof has two sides, so double the tiles; the ridge you count only once.

Pitch in degrees and percent

Drawings and products don't always use the same unit. The percent is the rise per metre of run:

Minimum pitch per material

Most clay and concrete tiles need a pitch of at least 15 to 25 degrees, depending on the tile; the manufacturer gives the exact minimum. EPDM and bitumen are for flat roofs, which still need a fall of about 1.5 to 2 cm per metre so water drains.

Common mistakes

Using the floor area: a sloping roof is bigger than the floor under it. At 45° the roof area is 41% larger (1 ÷ cos 45° = 1.41).

Forgetting the overhang: add the eaves to the run and the verges to the width before you calculate.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate roof pitch?

Divide the rise by the run and take the arctangent. A rise of 300 cm over a run of 500 cm is a pitch of about 31°.

How many roof tiles per m²?

It depends on the tile model. Large concrete tiles need about 10 per m², small clay tiles can need 15 or more. Use the number from the manufacturer.

What batten spacing should I use?

The batten spacing follows from the tile length and the minimum overlap the manufacturer specifies, often around 30–35 cm.

How do I get the roof area from the floor area?

Divide by the cosine of the pitch. 100 m² of floor under a 35° roof gives 100 ÷ cos 35° = 122 m² of roof.

What is a 30 degree roof pitch in percent?

tan(30°) × 100 = 58%, so the roof rises 58 cm per metre.

How many roof tiles are in a square metre?

It depends on the tile: large interlocking tiles are about 10 per m², smaller tiles more. The manufacturer gives the number per batten spacing.
