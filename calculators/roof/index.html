How the roof pitch calculator works

Enter the rise and the run of the roof in centimetres. The pitch angle follows from the ratio between the two, and the slope length from Pythagoras. Multiply the slope length by the roof width and you have the roof area.

Choose tiles, bitumen, EPDM or metal for the material estimate. For tiles you also get battens (rows by batten spacing) and ridge tiles (about 3 per metre of ridge). Cut waste is added on top.

Margin tip: 10% for a simple gable roof. Hips, valleys and dormers need 15% or more.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate roof pitch?

Divide the rise by the run and take the arctangent. A rise of 300 cm over a run of 500 cm is a pitch of about 31°.

How many roof tiles per m²?

It depends on the tile model. Large concrete tiles need about 10 per m², small clay tiles can need 15 or more. Use the number from the manufacturer.

What batten spacing should I use?

The batten spacing follows from the tile length and the minimum overlap the manufacturer specifies, often around 30–35 cm.
