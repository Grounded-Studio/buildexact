How the sheet material calculator works

Enter the size of the wall, floor or roof and subtract openings. Pick a common sheet size or type your own in mm.

The calculator adds waste and divides by the area of one sheet, rounded up to whole sheets. It also gives the number of screws, so you can buy everything in one go.

Tip: lay sheets staggered like bricks. You lose less material and the floor or wall is stronger.

Worked example: a wall of 5 × 2.6 m

A wall of 5 m long and 2.6 m high, no openings, sheets of 2440 × 1220 mm, 10% waste and 30 screws per sheet:

Area5 × 2.6 = 13.0 m²
With 10% waste13.0 × 1.10 = 14.3 m²
One sheet2.44 × 1.22 = 2.98 m²
Sheets14.3 ÷ 2.98 = 4.8 → 5 sheets
Screws5 × 30 = 150
Left over5 × 2.98 − 13.0 = 1.9 m²

So you buy 5 sheets and a box of 200 screws. The leftover is enough to cut a few strips for the edges.

Which sheet for which job

Plywood is strong and holds screws well, so it is used for floors, roofs and anything that has to carry weight. OSB is cheaper and just as strong in one direction, popular for walls and subfloors. MDF is smooth and stable, but only for dry indoor use. Plasterboard is for walls and ceilings that you finish and paint.

For floors choose tongue-and-groove sheets: the edges lock together, so you need fewer joists under the joints.

Plan the layout

Start against the longest straight wall and lay the sheets staggered, like brickwork. Joints then never line up and the cut-offs of one row often fit at the start of the next. Large cut-outs, such as a staircase or a hatch, are best drawn on paper first; they can cost you an extra sheet.

Frequently asked questions

How many sheets of plywood do I need?

Area plus 10% waste, divided by the area of one sheet. A wall of 5 × 2.6 m (13 m²) needs 5 sheets of 2440 × 1220 mm.

What size is a sheet of OSB?

The most common size is 2440 × 1220 mm (2.98 m²). Tongue-and-groove floor sheets are often 2440 × 590 or 2500 × 675 mm.

How many screws per sheet?

About 25 to 35 per standard sheet: every 15-20 cm along the edges and every 30 cm on the studs in between.

How many sheets of plasterboard for a ceiling?

Ceiling area plus 10%, divided by the sheet area. A ceiling of 4 × 3 m needs 13.2 ÷ 3.12 = 4.2, so 5 sheets of 2600 × 1200 mm.

How much waste should I count for sheet material?

About 10% for simple rectangles, 15% or more with many corners, slopes or cut-outs.
