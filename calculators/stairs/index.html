How the stair calculator works

Enter the total floor-to-floor height and the rise you would like per step. The calculator divides the height by that rise and rounds to a whole number of steps, then shows the real rise, the going and the slope of the stair.

The stringer length follows from the height and the total run. For straight, L- and U-shaped stairs you also get the handrail length and the number of balusters at the spacing you choose.

Comfort tip: aim for a rise of 16–19 cm and a tread of 25–30 cm. A common check is 2 × rise + tread ≈ 63 cm.

Frequently asked questions

How many steps does my stair need?

Divide the total height by your target rise. 280 cm ÷ 17 cm = 16.5, so 16 or 17 steps. The calculator rounds and shows the exact rise per step.

What is a comfortable stair rise?

Between about 16 and 19 cm. Lower is more comfortable but needs more room; higher is steeper. Check the maximum allowed by your local building rules.

How far apart should balusters be?

Close enough that a child's head cannot pass between them; many rules use a maximum gap of about 10–12 cm.
