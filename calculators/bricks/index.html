How the brick calculator works

Enter the wall length and height, then subtract openings for doors and windows. The calculator multiplies the net wall area by the number of bricks per m² for your brick size, adds cut waste and rounds up to whole bricks.

Mortar is calculated in kilograms per m² and turned into bags, and for a cavity wall you can add wall ties per m². Lintels over openings are listed separately so you don't forget them on the order.

Margin tip: 5% extra covers breakage and cuts on a plain wall. Walls with many openings or corners need closer to 10%.

Worked example: a garden wall with two openings

A wall of 8 × 2.5 m with a window of 1.5 × 1.2 m and a door of 0.9 × 2.1 m, bricks of 210 × 100 × 50 mm at 76 per m², 5% waste, 35 kg mortar per m²:

Gross area8 × 2.5 = 20 m²
Openings1.80 + 1.89 = 3.69 m²
Net area20 − 3.69 = 16.31 m²
Bricks16.31 × 76 × 1.05 = 1301.5 → 1302 bricks
Mortar16.31 × 35 = 571 kg → 23 bags of 25 kg
Lintels1.5 + 0.4 = 1.9 m and 0.9 + 0.4 = 1.3 m

Each lintel is 40 cm longer than its opening, so it rests 20 cm on the brickwork at both ends.

Half-brick or full-brick wall

The bricks per m² are for a half-brick wall, one brick thick. A full-brick wall, two bricks thick, needs twice as many bricks and more than twice the mortar, because of the joint between the two leaves. A cavity wall is two separate leaves: count the outer leaf in bricks and the inner leaf in blocks.

Wall ties in a cavity wall

Wall ties connect the two leaves. The calculator uses 5 per m² by default; follow the number on the drawings or in your local code, and add extra ties along openings and at the top of the wall.

Common mistakes

Mixing batches: bricks differ in colour per batch, so order everything at once and mix from several pallets while laying.

Forgetting the joint: the bricks per m² depend on the joint. At 12 mm instead of 10 mm you need a few bricks per m² fewer, but more mortar.

Laying in frost: don't lay bricks below about 5 °C, and cover fresh work against rain and frost.

Frequently asked questions

How many bricks are in one m²?

It depends on the brick size and joint. Common sizes need between about 50 and 75 bricks per m² for a single-skin wall. Check the number on the supplier's data sheet.

How much mortar do I need per m² of brickwork?

Roughly 25–40 kg of dry mortar per m², depending on brick size and joint thickness. The calculator uses 35 kg/m² by default.

Do I subtract doors and windows?

Yes. Enter the openings so you only pay for the wall you actually build, then add lintels above each opening.

How many bricks are in a square metre?

It depends on the brick and the joint. With a 10 mm joint: about 76 bricks of 210 × 100 × 50 mm, or about 59 of the UK size 215 × 102.5 × 65 mm.

How much mortar do I need per m²?

Around 30 to 40 kg per m² for a half-brick wall. The calculator uses 35 kg by default; change it to what your mortar bag says.
