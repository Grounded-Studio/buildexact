How the brick calculator works

Enter the wall length and height, then subtract openings for doors and windows. The calculator multiplies the net wall area by the number of bricks per m² for your brick size, adds cut waste and rounds up to whole bricks.

Mortar is calculated in kilograms per m² and turned into bags, and for a cavity wall you can add wall ties per m². Lintels over openings are listed separately so you don't forget them on the order.

Margin tip: 5% extra covers breakage and cuts on a plain wall. Walls with many openings or corners need closer to 10%.

Frequently asked questions

How many bricks are in one m²?

It depends on the brick size and joint. Common sizes need between about 50 and 75 bricks per m² for a single-skin wall. Check the number on the supplier's data sheet.

How much mortar do I need per m² of brickwork?

Roughly 25–40 kg of dry mortar per m², depending on brick size and joint thickness. The calculator uses 35 kg/m² by default.

Do I subtract doors and windows?

Yes. Enter the openings so you only pay for the wall you actually build, then add lintels above each opening.
