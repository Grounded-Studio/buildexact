How the tile calculator works

Enter the area you want to tile and the tile size. The calculator adds the grout joint to every tile, works out how many tiles cover the area, then adds cut waste for the laying pattern you pick: 10% for straight or half-brick, 15% for diagonal and 20% for herringbone.

Tiles are always rounded up to whole tiles and whole boxes, and you can add one spare box for later repairs. Adhesive is calculated per m² and turned into bags, and an optional edge length gives you the trim you need.

Margin tip: keep at least 10% extra, and order everything from one batch. Tiles from a later batch can differ slightly in colour.

Frequently asked questions

How many tiles do I need for 10 m²?

With 60×60 cm tiles and a 3 mm joint you need about 28 tiles for 10 m², and roughly 31 with 10% cut waste. Enter your own tile size for an exact figure.

How much cut waste should I add for tiles?

10% for a straight or half-brick pattern, 15% for diagonal and around 20% for herringbone. Small rooms with many corners need a little more.

How much tile adhesive do I need per m²?

Usually 4 to 7 kg per m², depending on tile size and the notched trowel. Large tiles need more. The calculator uses 6 kg/m² by default; change it to what your adhesive bag says.
