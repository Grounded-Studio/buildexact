How the unit converter works

Pick what you convert, type a value and choose its unit. You see the value in every other unit at once, so you never have to look up a factor.

Slope converts between degrees, percent and a ratio of 1:x, and shows the rise per metre. Handy for ramps, roofs and the fall of drains and terraces.

Tip: a fall of 1:100 is 1% or 10 mm per metre, which is the usual minimum for a terrace.

Frequently asked questions

How many square feet is 1 m²?

1 m² is 10.764 square feet. 1 square foot is 0.0929 m².

How do I convert a slope in degrees to percent?

Percent = tan(degrees) × 100. 30° is 57.7%, 45° is 100%.

What does a slope of 1:50 mean?

1 unit up for every 50 units across: 2% or 20 mm per metre.
