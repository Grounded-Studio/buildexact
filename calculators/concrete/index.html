How the concrete calculator works

Enter length, width and depth (in cm) or start from a preset for a floor, foundation or driveway. Volume = length × width × depth, plus a waste percentage for spillage, uneven ground and formwork that bulges slightly.

For ready-mix the order is rounded up to the next half cubic metre, the way most suppliers deliver. For self-mixing you get the number of 25 kg cement bags (about 14 per m³). Optional rebar is calculated in kilograms per m³ and converted into the number of bars of the length you buy.

Margin tip: add 5–10%. Running short halfway through a pour is far more expensive than a small surplus, because a second delivery leaves a cold joint.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate cubic metres of concrete?

Multiply length × width × depth in metres. A slab of 5 × 4 m and 12 cm thick is 5 × 4 × 0.12 = 2.4 m³, plus waste.

Should I order ready-mix or mix it myself?

Below roughly 1 m³ mixing yourself is often cheaper and easier. Above that, a ready-mix truck saves a lot of work and gives a more even quality.

How thick should a concrete floor be?

A garden path or shed base is often 8–10 cm, a garage floor or driveway 12–15 cm. Foundations depend on the load; follow the design or local building rules.
