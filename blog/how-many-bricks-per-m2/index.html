How Many Bricks per m² Do I Need? Bricks and Mortar for Your Wall

🏗️ Calculations · 📅 November 14, 2026 ⏱ 6 min read ✍️ Grounded Studio

Order too few bricks and you wait for a second delivery that may not match the colour of the first. Order too many and you are left with pallets in the garden. With the size of your brick and the joint width you can work out exactly how many you need per m².

The formula

Every brick takes up its own size plus one joint in length and in height. For a single-leaf wall, one brick thick:

Bricks per m² = 1 ÷ ((length + joint) × (height + joint)), with all sizes in metres.

Example for a 215 × 102.5 × 65 mm brick with 10 mm joints: 1 ÷ (0.225 × 0.075) = 59.3 bricks per m². That is why you often hear "60 bricks per m²".

These numbers are for a wall one brick thick (a half-brick wall), without waste. A wall two bricks thick needs about twice as many.

Step by step for your wall

  1. Measure the wall: length × height = gross area.
  2. Subtract openings: width × height of every window and door.
  3. Multiply the net area by the bricks per m² for your brick.
  4. Add waste: 5% for straightforward work, up to 10% with lots of cutting or reclaimed bricks.

Worked example

A wall of 6.0 × 2.5 m with a window of 1.2 × 1.0 m and a door of 0.9 × 2.1 m, built with 215 × 102.5 × 65 bricks:

  • Gross: 6.0 × 2.5 = 15.00 m²
  • Openings: 1.20 + 1.89 = 3.09 m²
  • Net: 15.00 − 3.09 = 11.91 m²
  • Bricks: 11.91 × 60 = 715, plus 5% waste = 751 bricks

How much mortar?

For a half-brick wall the joints come to about 18 to 20 litres of mortar per m². In dry mix that is roughly 30 to 40 kg per m², depending on the brick, the joints and how much you lose. The brick calculator starts at 35 kg per m², which you can change. For the example wall: 11.91 × 35 = 417 kg, or 17 bags of 25 kg. The bag always says how much mortar it makes, so check it.

Lintels and wall ties

  • Lintels: every opening needs one, longer than the opening so it rests on the brickwork on both sides. The calculator adds 20 cm per side. A 1.2 m window then needs a 1.6 m lintel.
  • Wall ties: in a cavity wall the two leaves are tied together. The number per m² depends on local rules and the engineer's spec, usually a few per m² plus extra around openings. The calculator starts at 5 per m².

💡 Order from one batch

Bricks from different production runs can differ slightly in colour. Order everything in one go, including the waste, and mix bricks from several pallets as you lay them.

Calculate it in seconds

With the brick calculator you enter the wall, the openings and the bricks per m², and you get the bricks, mortar bags, lintels and wall ties. You can try it free online, without logging in. In the BuildExact app you find it under Instant calculations → Brick calculator.

Summary

Bricks per m² = 1 ÷ ((length + joint) × (height + joint)). For a 215 × 65 mm brick with 10 mm joints that is about 60 per m². Subtract openings, add 5 to 10% waste, count on about 35 kg of mortar per m² and give every opening a lintel that is 40 cm longer.
